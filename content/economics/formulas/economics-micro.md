# Microeconomics Formulas

## Elasticity of Demand

**Own-price elasticity**:
$$E^d_{P_x} = \frac{\%\Delta Q^d_x}{\%\Delta P_x} = (Q_d)'_P \times \frac{P_x}{Q^d_x}$$
where $(Q_d)'_P$ = slope of demand function (not demand curve)

**Income elasticity**:
$$E^d_I = \frac{\%\Delta Q^d_x}{\%\Delta I}$$

**Cross-price elasticity**:
$$E^d_{P_y} = \frac{\%\Delta Q^d_x}{\%\Delta P_y}$$

## Cost Functions

$$TC = TFC + TVC$$
$$MC = \frac{\Delta TC}{\Delta Q}$$
$$AFC = \frac{TFC}{Q}, \quad AVC = \frac{TVC}{Q}, \quad ATC = \frac{TC}{Q}$$

## Revenue Functions

$$TR = \sum(P_i \times Q_i)$$
$$AR = \frac{TR}{Q}$$
$$MR = \frac{\Delta TR}{\Delta Q} = P\left(1 + \frac{1}{\text{elasticity}}\right)$$

**MR and Demand relationship** (linear demand $Q_D = a - bP$):
- Demand: $P = \frac{a - Q_D}{b}$, slope = $-1/b$
- $MR = \frac{a - 2Q_D}{b}$, slope = $-2/b$ (twice as steep)

## Profit Maximization

$$\text{Profit max at } MR = MC$$

**Breakeven**: $TR = TC$ or $AR = ATC$

**Shutdown (SR)**: $TR < TVC$ or $AR < AVC$

## Concentration Measures

**N-firm concentration ratio**: $CR_N = \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i$

**Herfindahl-Hirschman Index**: $HHI = \sum_{i=1}^{N} s_i^2$

where $s_i$ = market share of firm $i$
