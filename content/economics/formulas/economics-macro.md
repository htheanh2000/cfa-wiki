# Macroeconomics Formulas

## GDP

**Expenditure approach**:
$$GDP = C + I + G + (X - M)$$

**Income approach**:
$$GDP = \text{National income} + \text{Capital consumption allowance} + \text{Statistical discrepancy}$$

## GDP Deflator

$$\text{GDP deflator}_t = \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}_t}{\text{Real GDP}_t} \times 100$$

$$\text{Real GDP}_t = \frac{\text{Nominal GDP}_t}{\text{GDP deflator}_t} \times 100$$

## Saving-Investment Identity

$$S = I + (G - T) + (X - M)$$

$$GDP = C + S + T = C + I + G + (X - M)$$

## Fiscal Balance

$$(G - T) = (S - I) + (M - X)$$

A government deficit must be financed by excess private saving or a trade deficit.

## Economic Growth

**Production function**:
$$Y = A \times f(L, K)$$

**Growth accounting**:
$$\frac{\Delta Y}{Y} = \frac{\Delta A}{A} + \alpha\frac{\Delta K}{K} + (1-\alpha)\frac{\Delta L}{L}$$

where $A$ = total factor productivity, $\alpha$ = capital's share

**Compounded annual growth rate**:
$$g = \left(\frac{Y_t}{Y_0}\right)^{1/t} - 1$$

## Fiscal Multiplier

$$\text{Multiplier} = \frac{1}{1 - MPC(1-t)}$$

where $MPC$ = marginal propensity to consume, $t$ = tax rate
