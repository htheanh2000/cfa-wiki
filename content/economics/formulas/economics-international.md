# International Economics Formulas

## GDP vs. GNP

$$GNP = GDP + \text{Net factor income from abroad}$$

## Balance of Payments

$$\text{Current Account} + \text{Capital Account} + \text{Financial Account} = 0$$

## Cross Rates

$$\frac{A}{C} = \frac{A}{B} \times \frac{B}{C}$$

## Covered Interest Rate Parity

$$\frac{F_{A/B}}{S_{A/B}} = \frac{1 + r_A}{1 + r_B}$$

where $F$ = forward exchange rate, $S$ = spot exchange rate, $r_A$, $r_B$ = interest rates for currencies A and B

## Forward Premium/Discount

$$\text{Forward premium (discount)} = \frac{F - S}{S} \times \frac{360}{\text{days}}$$

- Premium if $F > S$ (base currency appreciating)
- Discount if $F < S$ (base currency depreciating)

## Forward Rate from Points

$$F = S + \text{Forward points}$$

## Bid-Ask Spread

$$\text{Spread} = \text{Ask} - \text{Bid}$$
$$\text{Spread \%} = \frac{\text{Ask} - \text{Bid}}{\text{Ask}} \times 100$$
