# Glossary: M03 — Fiscal Policy

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Fiscal policy | Government decisions about taxation and spending to influence the economy |
| Monetary policy | Central bank activities to influence the quantity of money and credit |
| Government deficit | Government expenditures exceed revenues over a period |
| Structural budget deficit | Deficit that would exist if economy were at full employment |
| Transfer payments | Government payments to individuals (e.g., unemployment benefits, pensions) |
| Crowding out | Government borrowing raises interest rates, reducing private investment |
| Ricardian equivalence | Theory that government borrowing is offset by increased private saving |
| Expansionary fiscal policy | ↑Government spending or ↓taxes to stimulate AD |
| Contractionary fiscal policy | ↓Government spending or ↑taxes to reduce AD |
| Recognition lag | Delay in identifying current economic conditions |
| Action lag | Delay in implementing policy changes |
| Impact lag | Delay between policy implementation and its economic effect |
| Cyclically adjusted budget deficit | Budget deficit adjusted for the effects of the business cycle |
| Automatic stabilizers | Tax and spending provisions that automatically counter business cycle |
| Fiscal multiplier | Ratio of change in GDP to change in government spending |
