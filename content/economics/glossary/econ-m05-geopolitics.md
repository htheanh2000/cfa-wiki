# Glossary: M05 — Introduction to Geopolitics

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Geopolitics | Study of how geography, power, and politics influence international relations |
| Globalization | Increasing economic interdependence among countries through trade, capital flows, and technology |
| Sanctions | Restrictions imposed on a country to influence its behavior |
| Soft power | Ability to influence through culture, values, and diplomacy rather than force |
| Hard power | Influence through military force or economic coercion |
| Resource nationalism | Government assertion of control over natural resources |
| Political risk | Risk of loss due to political instability or policy changes |
| Geopolitical risk | Risk from geopolitical events affecting investments (war, sanctions, regime change) |
