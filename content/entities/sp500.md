# S&P 500

**Type**: Equity market index
**Coverage**: 500 largest US publicly traded companies
**Use in CFA**: Primary benchmark for US equity returns

## Role in Quantitative Methods

- Used as independent variable ($X$) in regression examples
- **ABC stock regression**: $\hat{ABC} = -2.3\% + 0.64 \times \text{S\&P 500 excess return}$
  - Slope = 0.64 → ABC moves 0.64% for every 1% move in S&P 500
  - This slope is essentially the stock's **beta** ($\beta$)
- Used to demonstrate [[concepts/correlation|correlation]], [[concepts/covariance|covariance]], and [[modules/quantitative-methods/m10-simple-linear-regression/index|regression analysis]]
