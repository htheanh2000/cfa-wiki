## Topics Covered

1. **Instrument Features & Cash Flows** (M01–M02): Bond characteristics, coupon structures, repayment mechanisms
2. **Markets & Issuance** (M03–M05): Primary/secondary markets, corporate and government issuers
3. **Valuation & Yields** (M06–M09): Bond pricing, yield measures, term structure
4. **Risk Measures** (M10–M13): Duration, convexity, curve-based risk
5. **Credit Analysis** (M14–M16): Credit risk, government and corporate credit analysis
6. **Securitization** (M17–M19): ABS, MBS, securitization structures
