# Glossary: FI Module 04 — Corporate Issuers

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Corporate bond** | A bond issued by a corporation to raise capital; may be secured or unsecured |
| **Debenture** | An unsecured corporate bond backed only by the issuer's general creditworthiness |
| **Commercial paper (CP)** | Short-term unsecured promissory note issued by corporations, typically with maturity ≤ 270 days |
| **Medium-term note (MTN)** | A corporate debt instrument issued continuously under a shelf registration, with maturities typically from 9 months to 30+ years |
| **Syndicated loan** | A loan provided by a group (syndicate) of lenders, structured and administered by one or more arranging banks |
| **Secured bond** | A bond backed by a pledge of specific assets as collateral |
| **Unsecured bond** | A bond backed only by the issuer's general credit; no specific collateral |
| **First mortgage bond** | A bond secured by a first-priority lien on real property |
| **Equipment trust certificate** | A bond secured by specific equipment (e.g., aircraft, rolling stock) |
| **Collateral trust bond** | A bond secured by financial assets (stocks, bonds) held by a trustee |
