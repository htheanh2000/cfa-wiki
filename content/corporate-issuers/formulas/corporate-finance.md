# Corporate Finance Formulas

## Working Capital and Liquidity

**Cash Conversion Cycle**:
$$CCC = DOH + DSO - DPO$$

**Days of Inventory on Hand**:
$$DOH = \frac{\text{Inventory}}{\text{COGS}/365}$$

**Days Sales Outstanding**:
$$DSO = \frac{\text{Accounts receivable}}{\text{Revenue}/365}$$

**Days Payable Outstanding**:
$$DPO = \frac{\text{Accounts payable}}{\text{COGS}/365}$$

**Current Ratio**:
$$\text{Current ratio} = \frac{\text{Current assets}}{\text{Current liabilities}}$$

**Quick Ratio**:
$$\text{Quick ratio} = \frac{\text{Cash + Short-term marketable securities + Receivables}}{\text{Current liabilities}}$$

## Capital Budgeting

**Net Present Value (NPV)**:
$$NPV = \sum_{t=0}^{n} \frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t}$$

where $CF_0$ = initial outlay (negative), $CF_t$ = after-tax cash flow, $r$ = required rate of return

**Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**:
$$0 = \sum_{t=0}^{n} \frac{CF_t}{(1+IRR)^t}$$

**Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)**:
$$ROIC = \frac{\text{After-tax operating profit}}{\text{Average book value of invested capital}}$$

**Break-even (units)**:
$$\text{Break-even} = \frac{\text{Fixed costs}}{\text{Contribution margin per unit}}$$

## Capital Structure

**Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)**:
$$WACC = w_d \times r_d \times (1-t) + w_e \times r_e + w_p \times r_p$$

**After-tax cost of debt**:
$$r_d(1-t)$$

**CAPM (Cost of equity)**:
$$r_e = R_F + \beta_i[E(R_M) - R_F]$$

**Bond Yield Plus Risk Premium (BYPRP)**:
$$r_e = r_d + \text{Risk premium}$$

**Modigliani-Miller with taxes**:
$$V_L = V_U + t \times D$$

**MM Proposition II (with taxes)**:
$$r_e = r_0 + (r_0 - r_d)(1-t) \times \frac{D}{E}$$

## Leverage Effect on ROE
$$ROE = \frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Equity}}$$

Debt amplifies ROE variance: higher upside when ROA > cost of debt, larger downside when ROA < cost of debt.
