# Glossary: M02 — Investors and Stakeholders

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Stakeholder | Any individual or group with a vested interest in a company |
| Shareholder theory | Corporate governance focused on maximizing shareholder value |
| Stakeholder theory | Broader governance balancing interests of all stakeholder groups |
| Residual claim | Shareholders' claim on net assets after all liabilities are settled |
| Financial leverage | Use of debt to amplify returns (and risk) on equity |
| ESG factors | Environmental, Social, and Governance considerations in investing |
| Stranded assets | Assets that lose value due to environmental or regulatory changes |
| Negative externality | Cost imposed on third parties not directly involved in a transaction |
