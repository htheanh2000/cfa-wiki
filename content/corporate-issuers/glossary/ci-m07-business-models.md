# Glossary: M07 — Business Models

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Business model | How a company generates revenue and profits |
| Value proposition | What the company offers to customers and why they choose it |
| Value chain | Internal activities that create value for customers |
| Supply chain | All steps in creating a product, both within and external to the firm |
| Unit economics | Revenue and costs expressed on a per-unit basis |
| Contribution margin | Revenue per unit minus variable cost per unit |
| Break-even point | Output level where total revenue equals total costs |
| Bundling | Selling multiple products/services together |
| Razors-and-blades | Low initial price, high consumable price |
| Freemium | Free basic service, paid premium features |
| Penetration pricing | Low price to build scale and market share |
| Dynamic pricing | Prices vary by time or demand |
| Subscription model | Recurring payment for ongoing access to product/service |
| Franchising | Licensing business model to operate in a territory |
| Omnichannel | Integrating both physical and digital distribution channels |
