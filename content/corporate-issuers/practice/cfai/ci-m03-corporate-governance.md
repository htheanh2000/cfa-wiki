# CFAI Practice: M03 — Corporate Governance

*Source: [[corporate-issuers/sources/cfai-ci-practice-2026|CFAI Official Practice Problems 2026]]*
*Case: Kobe Steel Ltd. — falsifying data on materials quality*

## Questions

**1.** Three key stakeholder relationships and their role in misconduct:
> - **Kobe Corp ↔ Customers** (contractual): Delivered inferior-quality product
> - **BOD ↔ Kobe Corp** (agent): Board failed to monitor, causing reputational damage
> - **Managers ↔ Employees** (contractual): Culture prioritized winning orders over quality

**2.** Stakeholder incentive conflicts:
> Customers incentivized by product quality; management incentivized by winning purchase orders and meeting deadlines → quality standard conflict.

**3.** Best practice to prevent recurrence:
- A. Shareholder rights plan
- B. Stock-based compensation plan
- **C. Audit committee of independent members** ✓

**4.** Financial risk implications:
> Stock price fell + cost of debt jumped. Investors must assess past benefit from misconduct and future loss from (1) no longer falsifying data, (2) customer confidence loss.

**5.** Governance failures most likely resulted in reduced:
- A. cost of debt
- B. agency costs
- **C. growth opportunities** ✓

> Reputational damage to Kobe among customers reduces growth opportunities.
