### Key Concepts by Module

- **M01-M02**: Quantitative portfolio theory (MPT, CAPM, performance measures)
- **M03-M04**: Portfolio management process and construction (IPS, asset allocation)
- **M05**: Behavioral biases (cognitive errors, emotional biases)
- **M06**: Risk management framework and tools

### Exam Weight

Portfolio Management typically represents **5-8%** of the CFA Level 1 exam.
