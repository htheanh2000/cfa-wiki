# Glossary: M04 — Overview of Equity Securities

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **American Depositary Receipt (ADR)** | A negotiable certificate representing shares of a foreign company, traded on a US exchange |
| **Book Value** | The net asset value of a company as reported on its balance sheet (assets minus liabilities) |
| **Book Value per Share (BVPS)** | Common shareholders' equity divided by shares outstanding |
| **Callable Common Stock** | Common shares that the issuing company can repurchase at a predetermined price |
| **Common Stock** | Equity securities representing residual ownership in a company, with voting rights |
| **Convertible Preferred Stock** | Preferred stock that can be converted into common shares at a specified ratio |
| **Cumulative Preferred Stock** | Preferred stock where unpaid dividends accumulate and must be paid before common dividends |
| **Depository Receipt (DR)** | A certificate representing ownership of shares in a foreign company |
| **Dividend** | A distribution of earnings to shareholders, typically in cash or additional shares |
| **DuPont Analysis** | Decomposition of ROE into profit margin, asset turnover, and financial leverage |
| **Global Depository Receipt (GDR)** | A depository receipt issued outside the company's home country and the US |
| **Global Registered Share** | A common share traded on stock exchanges worldwide in different currencies |
| **Growth Stock** | A stock expected to grow earnings faster than the market average; typically high P/E |
| **Market Capitalization** | The total market value of a company's outstanding shares ($P \times Q$) |
| **Non-Cumulative Preferred Stock** | Preferred stock where unpaid dividends do not accumulate |
| **Participating Preferred Stock** | Preferred stock that shares in profits beyond its fixed dividend rate |
| **Preferred Stock** | Equity with fixed dividend payments and priority over common stock in liquidation |
| **Private Equity** | Equity investments in companies not listed on public exchanges |
| **Putable Common Stock** | Common shares that the holder can sell back to the issuer at a predetermined price |
| **Return on Equity (ROE)** | Net income divided by average shareholders' equity; measures profitability |
| **Share Repurchase (Buyback)** | A company purchasing its own shares from the market, reducing shares outstanding |
| **Sponsored DR** | A depository receipt where the foreign company is involved in the issuance |
| **Unsponsored DR** | A depository receipt created without the direct involvement of the foreign company |
| **Value Stock** | A stock trading at a low price relative to fundamentals (low P/E, P/B); considered undervalued |
| **Voting Rights** | The right of common shareholders to vote on corporate matters |
