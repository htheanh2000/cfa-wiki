# Ethical and Professional Standards

## Overview

Ethical and Professional Standards is a core topic area in the CFA Level 1 curriculum. It covers the CFA Institute Code of Ethics, Standards of Professional Conduct, and the Global Investment Performance Standards (GIPS). This topic typically represents 15-20% of the exam.

## Key Themes

1. **Trust and professionalism** are the foundation of the investment industry
2. The **Code of Ethics** sets aspirational principles; the **Standards** set minimum benchmarks
3. **Guidance for Standards I-VII** provides detailed application with real-world scenarios
4. **GIPS** ensures fair, comparable performance reporting across firms
5. **Ethics Application** tests the ability to apply standards to realistic case studies

## Study Strategy

- Master the Code of Ethics and all 7 Standards first (M01-M02)
- Study the detailed guidance with practice examples (M03) -- this is the largest and most heavily tested module
- Understand GIPS at a conceptual level (M04)
- Practice case-study-style questions extensively (M05)
