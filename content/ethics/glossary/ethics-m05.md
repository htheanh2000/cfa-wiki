# Glossary: Ethics Application

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Ethics application** | The process of applying the Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct to real-world scenarios and case studies on the CFA exam |
| **Case study approach** | A method of testing ethics knowledge by presenting a realistic professional scenario and asking candidates to identify violations and appropriate actions |
| **Scenario analysis** | Evaluating a described situation to determine which Standards apply, whether violations occurred, and what corrective action is required |
| **Standard identification** | The skill of reading a scenario and correctly identifying which specific Standard(s) of Professional Conduct are relevant or have been violated |
| **Guidance application** | Using the official CFA Institute guidance, recommended procedures, and examples to determine appropriate professional behavior in specific situations |
